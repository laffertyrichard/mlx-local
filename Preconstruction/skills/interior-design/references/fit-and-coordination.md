# Fit, evidence and room operations

This is a design protocol synthesized from the research. Source keys resolve in [sources.md](sources.md); dimensional guidelines are dated recommendations, not automatic law or user-specific comfort guarantees.

## Evidence and authority

Keep an evidence register: ID, file/revision/page/view or measurement/date, decision scope, datum (stud/finish/rough/clear), units, confidence, conflict/question and resolution. Written, calculated, scaled, typological and field-measured values are distinct. Do not scale an uncalibrated image, assume door width, average incompatible dimensions or silently stretch the shell.

Apply current owner decisions to their explicit choices. Approved architectural documents establish the documented envelope/space planning; actual current field geometry still needs verification. Interior elevations/specifications control their interior intent. Adopted shops describe fabrication coordinated within that space. A shop signature or newer date does not settle an ID/appearance/layout conflict. Identify the scope of each approval and who can resolve a genuine conflict. Owner aesthetic choices do not waive applicable law or establish concealed conditions.

Use a small question ledger when handing off: Q-ID, source evidence, affected object/decision/quantity IDs, answerable question, decision-maker, provisional treatment, impact, status and answer/date. Supported work can proceed while dependent sizing remains open.

## The three envelopes

1. **Physical:** installed body, countertop/trim/handles and wall irregularity, not catalog nominal width alone.
2. **Operation:** full door/drawer travel, appliance opening angle, recliner/bed extension, moving hardware and clearances required by the specific manufacturer.
3. **Human use:** body/device approach, stance, reach, transfers, seated legs, occupied chairs, assistance and cleaning. Test with the actual people/device where relevant.

Overlay circulation and simultaneous uses: a passing route through an occupied work envelope may be unsuitable even if the door opens. Record which dimension is across the facing planes versus along the frontage. Verify side fillers, overhangs and service/removal access using current model-specific instructions [S5]. A taped/cardboard mockup is useful for uncertain comfort; it is not a compliance test.

For any cited numerical recommendation record source, edition/date, boundary definition and conditions. NKBA's public downloads distinguish work aisles, walkways, occupied-seat passing and landing/preparation space [S1–S4]; use the relevant guideline rather than one universal aisle number. The registry records conflicting edition labels and old referenced standards. Do not use the old PDFs' code references as current adopted law.

## Room tests (select relevant rows)

| Room | Operations and consequences to test |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Arrival/groceries → storage → wash/prep → cook → serve → clear; multiple cooks and visitor beverage routes; waste; fridge full opening; oven/dishwasher loading; landing/prep surfaces; extraction and power feasibility. A work triangle is a diagnostic, not a universal layout. |
| Bath/wet room | Entry privacy, toileting, dressing, controls before spray, towels, grooming light, dry storage and cleaning; glass/door use; rescue/assistance where relevant; floor/drain/slope/waterproofing transitions and ventilation. A central vanity must earn its footprint through demonstrated use, not be categorically banned. |
| Bedroom | Actual bed frame, exits for each sleeper, bedside controls, dressing with drawers open, quiet/blackout, night route and escape-opening access. Preserve potential escape function pending local review. |
| Living/dining | Conversation/viewing, tables within reach, chairs occupied/pulled out, routes through groups, guests, privacy, glare and acoustic goals. Table-edge clearance alone is not clear passage. |
| Work/study | User-adjustable posture, screen/inputs, legs/reach, glare, calls/privacy, cables and changing posture [S20–S22]. A shallow console is not automatically a full-day workstation. |
| Entry/storage/laundry | Inventory by internal dimensions and frequency; manageable heavy-item access; doors/drawers; hamper/folding; selected washer/dryer instructions, ventilation, leaks and maintenance. Gross cabinet volume is not usable storage. |

## Recommendation gate

Do not recommend a fixed purchase/layout while a known critical operation fails. Change a constrained feature and retest, rather than reducing width when depth is the cause. Confirm the product's installation, maintenance, wet/electrical ratings and interfaces before specification. Retained equipment is protected unless relocation is authorized. Distinguish unresolved data from a failed layout and explain the next actionable measurement, product sheet or decision.
