# Primary source registry

Research checked **2026-10-15**. These are original decision notes, not reproduced standards/manuals. Refresh the applicable regional product manual and project-specified edition before final decisions. IDs support handoffs; they do not imply a product has been selected.

| ID | Primary source and access | Use and limit |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | [AWI standards index](https://awinet.org/standards/) and [ANSI/AWI 0641-2019 introduction/scope](https://awinet.org/standards/architectural-wood-casework/introduction-foreword-1-0-2-0-architectural-wood-casework/), public text read | Casework aesthetic/performance scope; this standard excludes installation and integral building blocking/anchorage. Verify applicable document, grade/duty and contract. Industry standard is not automatically law. |
