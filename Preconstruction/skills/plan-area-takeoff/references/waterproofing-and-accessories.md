# Waterproofing and assembly accessories

Read supplied assembly details and product specifications. Reconcile the finish with its documented substrate, moisture-control layers and accessories. A finish tag alone does not establish an installation system, and typical practice does not establish project scope.

## Assembly coverage record

For each relevant wet area, roof, deck, exterior opening or other detailed assembly, link its parent ID to component rows with material/product, location/physical layer, source/detail, extent, quantity/basis, inclusion relationship, responsibility and unresolved question IDs. Use measured, provisional, by-others, excluded or not-established dispositions.

| Component | Separate basis |
|---|---|
| Backer / underlayment / mortar bed | Own surface, layers and documented thickness; not automatically equal to finish SF |
| Field membrane / vapor or air barrier | Its own continuous extent, opening policy and physical plane |
| Pans / curbs / upturns / penetrations | Supported faces or assembly EA, heights and depths |
| Seams / laps / reinforcing bands | Documented layout LF or added material SF, kept separate from installed coverage |
| Flashing / termination / drainage accessories | Actual LF/profile, corners/end dams/outlets EA where specified |
| Movement joints / sealants | Joint route LF, width/depth and product basis; separate from exposed finish trim |
| Testing / preparation | Documented operation/count or unquantified scope; no automatic material-area proxy |

Tile and waterproofing are separate layers: deducting a tiled niche opening does not remove waterproofing from its back and returns when the detail wraps them. Conversely, do not assume continuity beneath a bench, through an opening or behind a fixture without evidence. Preserve the assembly sequence and unresolved extent.

For sheet products, distinguish net installed coverage from lap/cut allowance and roll ordering. Do not add explicit lap material and an allowance that already includes those laps. For liquid products, derive volume only from stated applicable coverage, substrate and coat requirements; confirm whether coverage is per coat or for the complete system. Unknown coat/yield data prevents a defensible order quantity.

Calculate sealant volume from supported joint section × LF with consistent units, then use actual package yield and separate waste. Joint LF alone does not establish cartridges. Keep membrane field SF, seam LF and accessory EA as separate pricing quantities unless explicitly bundled.

Check changes to curbs, niches, benches, drains, skylights and roof/wall intersections against all affected components. Report missing or conflicting assembly details as questions. Do not design a waterproofing system or substitute an unapproved product to close a takeoff gap.
