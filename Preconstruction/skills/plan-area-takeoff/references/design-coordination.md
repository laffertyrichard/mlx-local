# Architectural and design coordination

## Source and scope coverage

Cross-reference the actual available files on every takeoff, including updates. Review architectural plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules alongside structural/MEP evidence, interior design decks/renderings, finish schedules, written scope and user decisions as applicable. Preserve requested page/phase/trade limits. Check references outside an excerpt when accessible and needed to interpret included work; flag unavailable references. Do not invent missing files or expand a limited flooring request into a full remodel estimate.

Record source title, revision/date, sheet/slide label and actual file page separately; excerpt pagination may differ from original slide numbers. Visually review relevant drawings, rendered images and embedded notes. Mark blank, divider and option pages explicitly. Preserve original selection text/product links and distinguish selected, option, TBD and procurement status; blank ordered/received fields mean unknown.

Use a callout coverage table with these REQUIRED fields:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Coverage ID / location | Stable row and physical assembly/room |
| Source evidence | File/revision plus sheet/detail, slide/page or schedule row |
| Callout and responsibility | Work, material/assembly, contractor/owner/designer/other responsibility |
| Quantity relationship | Quantity IDs; parent, component, alternative or reference-only relationship |
| Disposition | Measured, provisional, retained, excluded, by others, option or not established |
| Missing information | Unresolved dimensions, extent, selection, assembly or responsibility; question ID |

Map each relevant callout, including notes and details absent from the main floor plan. A single row may group repeated evidence for the same physical item; it must not conceal distinct unmeasured work. Audit in both directions: every source callout has a disposition, and every quantity has evidence. Keep page review separate from quantity completeness.

Apply supplied precedence and recorded decisions. Where none resolves a contradiction, state the estimating basis and retain the competing source. A user may choose a bid allowance or finish; that does not establish an engineered detail or utility approval. Continue supported work and isolate affected quantities instead of stopping the whole takeoff.

## Design work that must be reconciled

Within the requested scope, check these categories even when they appear only in elevations, illustrations or end notes:

- Decorative/faux beams, ridge pieces, wood/plank ceilings, coffers, arches and soffits. Separate decorative work from structural members. Carry run count and LF by profile; establish slope direction and section before converting projected LF to installed LF. Separate exposed-face/wrap SF and ceiling coverage SF. A photo establishes intent, not calibrated dimensions or whole-room extent. Wood finish does not automatically eliminate gypsum backing.
- Millwork runs, doors, operable drawers, false fronts, shelving, appliance panels and hardware. Count physical components once across alternative/repeated views. Front counts describe the cabinet assembly; they do not add another cabinet LF allowance. Use shop drawings for missing dimensions and interiors.
- Countertops, slab backsplashes, aprons, portals/arches, returns, ledges, benches, hearths and exposed edges. Keep fabrication classes separate. Establish curved profiles, depths and actual faces before SF/LF. An assembly EA can identify scope while fabrication SF remains unknown.
- Floor/wall tile, patterns, niches, trim, thresholds, bench occlusion and waterproofing. Assign every physical surface once. Separate gross wall fields from net installed finishes and separately exposed bench faces; do not add both gross and net alternatives.
- Paint, texture, plaster/limewash, wallpaper, trim/base, trimless openings, stair finishes and rail/guard work. Keep substrate and finish operations separate. Gross new-board SF is not automatically net paint SF or all existing repaint work.
- Decorative lighting, illuminated shelves, controls, pantry/hall notes, AV, drapery and appliance coordination. Rendered light locations do not establish fixture counts, strip LF or electrical distribution. Raise text-versus-image conflicts explicitly.

Separate plan bases from designer alternatives and selected work from options. Do not sum alternatives or count the same assembly once per discipline. Owner-furnished work still requires coordination of access, protection, backing, service connections and restoration where supported; identify responsibility and affected ordered/in-production items without automatically including all owner work in contractor totals.

## Questions ready for the user or an RFI

Create a stable question ID for each distinct decision. REQUIRED fields:

1. **Location and affected IDs:** room/assembly, coverage and quantity references.
2. **Evidence:** precise file/revision and sheet/detail/page/row references for each conflicting statement, or the missing source/dimension. Describe what each source actually establishes.
3. **Question:** direct, answerable wording requesting the governing selection, extent, dimension, detail or responsibility. Avoid a generic “please clarify.” Group dependent facts only when one decision resolves them.
4. **Decision-maker:** suggested designer, architect, engineer, owner, trade or utility; do not invent recipient identities.
5. **Current treatment and impact:** measured basis, explicit allowance/option, or quantity held; affected trades, fabrication/procurement and quantity/cost/schedule effect where supported. Unknown impact remains unknown.
6. **Status and resolution:** open, answered, partially answered or superseded; record the answer, source/date, estimating versus design-approval status, and affected document revisions. Leave dependencies open until actually resolved.

Example question: “A-3 calls for 8 × 4 in decorative beams; detail D/S-3 calls for 4 × 9 in. Which profile, material and exposed-face configuration should be priced? Current takeoff carries sourced centerline LF separately from structural framing; wrap SF remains held.” These example dimensions are not project defaults.

Present a concise prioritized question list to the user, with the full register in the requested package. Prioritize conflicts affecting structural interfaces, owner commitments, fabricated/ordered materials and major quantities. The user can answer directly or forward the wording; draft status is not an issued RFI, and no external message is authorized by this workflow.

When a reply or revised file arrives, cross-reference it against the open register. Close only what it resolves, retain the earlier basis for audit, and revise all dependent quantities, totals, scope descriptions, selections and overlays. Verify unaffected quantities remain unchanged. Claim complete coverage only when every applicable callout has a disposition; state separately which quantities remain provisional or unquantified.
