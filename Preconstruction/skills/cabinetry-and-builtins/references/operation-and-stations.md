# Operation, seated stations and appliances

## Moving assemblies

Use actual hardware SKU, manual/revision, hinge/plate combination, overlay/inset relationship, front thickness/profile, cup/drilling position, carcass thickness, handle projection, limits and rated loading. Draw front/box/handle sweeps and internal intrusion at closed, intermediate and full-open positions, including adjustment and installation tolerance. Test travel between states. A larger hinge angle alone does not establish internal pullout clearance; zero-protrusion is configuration-specific [S5–S6].

Test neighboring drawers/doors in the combinations the user needs, with hand access, contents access and occupied approach space. Quantify supplied envelope intersections in consistent local axes. A swept-envelope overlap identifies a coordination conflict; it may not prove two solids hit at the same instant. Explain that distinction and obtain actual moving geometry. If concurrent use is required, sequencing instructions or an interlock do not satisfy it. Relocate a bank, create a deliberate inactive corner, change the mechanism or revise the layout conditionally. Do not convert an idealized translation into a final filler/cut dimension without hardware and site data.

## Makeup stations and desks

Locate the user's station before locating the mirror: use the actual clear seating bay/occupant center, face/view distance and seated eye range. Do not use the full cabinet-run midpoint automatically. Dimension each variant independently from the same datum. Confirm mirror width/height and any adjustable magnifier with an actual user trial.

Use a section through the seated body: floor, chair/seat/arms, worktop top and underside, apron/drawer/rails/brackets, thigh/knee/toe width and depth, rear services/baseboard and chair withdrawal/circulation. Counter height alone proves none of these. Choose height and reach for the intended people/task and a mockup. ADA §306 is a useful shaped-clearance reference where relevant [S9]; verify legal applicability separately rather than imposing ADA on every private home.

Place facial task light to reveal the face with controlled glare/shadow, considering mirror edges, actual seated eye height, daylight and controls. Manufacturer lighting examples support product selection, not mandatory CCT/lumen values or health claims [S10]. Mock up light direction, reflection, color rendering and dimming with representative samples. Resolve electrical locations through the applicable trade/code workflow.

## Integrated appliances and storage types

For each appliance obtain the exact model/regional installation and panel manual. Track finished clear opening, hinge/panel/handle sweep, internal-drawer use/removal angle, panel thickness/finished weight and mounting pattern, ventilation path/free area, leveling/restraint, utilities and service extraction sequence. Nominal “36-inch” products are not interchangeable. Removable trim still blocks ventilation while installed unless its airflow treatment is approved. An inaccessible outlet or shutoff needs a valid access strategy, not cosmetic concealment.

For closets test usable hanging depth including doors/hinges/garments, pullouts, rods, reach and shelf access. For laundry coordinate appliance vibration/heat, hose/duct routing, ventilation, valves and equipment withdrawal against the selected manuals. For media/library units test heat dissipation, cable bend/access, removable equipment and articulating TV movement. For window seats test occupied seat/cushion height/depth, lid sweep/finger protection, load/support and reachable services; verify any window escape/access role before obstruction. Keep each requirement conditional on the actual activity, product and jurisdiction.
