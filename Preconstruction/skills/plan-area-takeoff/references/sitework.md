# Sitework and exterior improvements

Apply only to requested scope. Inventory civil, grading, drainage, utility, landscape and architectural site evidence; verify limits of work, property/easement context, survey datum and applicable sections. A building plan alone does not establish complete sitework quantities.

| Scope | Measurement and limitations |
|---|---|
| Clearing / demolition | Documented limits by material; separate retained/protected areas and salvaged items |
| Excavation / backfill | Routes/areas plus supported sections or existing/proposed surfaces; state volume condition |
| Paving / flatwork | Net SF by finish/section; each base, concrete/asphalt layer and thickness separately |
| Curbs / headers / edges | LF by section; concrete volume only from established section geometry |
| Drainage / site utilities | Located structures EA, depicted routes LF and documented elevations/slopes; no invented connections |
| Fences / gates / walls | Segment LF, height/type, gate EA, openings and documented foundations separately |
| Planting / irrigation | Located plants by type/size, material bed SF/CY, shown devices/routes only; schedule quantities reconciled to plan occurrences |

## Earthwork basis

Distinguish bank excavation, loose hauled material and compacted placed fill. Never equate them without sourced conversion factors. Record topsoil stripping, unsuitable-material removal, imported base, reuse suitability and disposal separately. Concrete footing volume is not excavation volume.

For a uniform supported trench, volume = route LF × section SF / 27 CY; record width, depth and any established slopes/working space. Variable sections require segmented calculations or sourced cross-sections. Do not invent trench slopes, shoring, overexcavation or utility depths.

For grading surfaces, validate common horizontal/vertical units and datum, matching boundaries, breaklines, coverage and holes. Compute existing-minus-proposed surface differences over the same supported area, separating cut from fill. Sparse spot elevations or contour labels alone do not justify a precise whole-site volume. Identify interpolation assumptions and gaps; a 2D area helper does not calculate terrain volumes.

Backfill follows excavation less documented permanent occupied volume in the same extent. Keep bedding and selected fill layers separate without counting them again as general backfill. Do not claim a balanced haul plan until reuse suitability and bank/loose/compacted conversions are established.

## Interfaces and coverage

Deduplicate sidewalks, patios and slabs appearing in both building and civil sets; assign one quantity owner. Scope exclusions for conditioned interior flooring do not automatically exclude separately requested exterior improvements.

Check utility trench restoration, paving cuts, drainage structures, fence returns and gate openings against their parent work. Separate drainage route quantity from hydraulic performance; plant count from procurement availability; and retaining-wall extent from engineered design. Carry missing sections, elevations, capacities and responsibilities as questions rather than zero quantities.

For site-wall or irregular hardscape scope, follow [irregular-sites-and-callout-audit.md](irregular-sites-and-callout-audit.md). Its segment ledger and curve/boundary evidence are required; published wall totals alone do not establish a new-work takeoff.
