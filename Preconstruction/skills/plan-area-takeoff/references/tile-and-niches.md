# Tile fields, edges and niches

## Elevation field SF

Trace each tile finish field by room and wall/location. Use elevation dimensions for height and actual steps/jogs. Deduct openings and surfaces assigned to other materials: glass doors, solid slab bench fronts, slab vanities, separate full-height mirrors and niche openings. A door symbol is not a tile hatch. For each distinct wall, deduct its own area displaced or occluded by the bench once. An end projection can exclude tile on that wall without establishing an additional exposed slab face. Deduplicate repeated views of the same physical wall.

Separate shower floors, shower walls, tub surrounds and vanity backsplashes. Decide and disclose whether tile continues behind surface-mounted mirrors/fixtures; an outlined mirror does not automatically mean omitted tile. Do not infer layout direction or product size solely from schematic hatch. Plain walls remain excluded when requested.

Render the NET tile polygon only. Keep glass and solid slab exclusions unfilled, preferably with dashed outlines and clear labels. Adding a red translucent deduction over a blue gross fill still makes the excluded object look tiled. If the old arithmetic was correct but the graphic was wrong, report a display correction rather than inventing a quantity reduction.

## Edge LF

Classify edge routes by room and treatment before totaling:

- Exposed field terminations.
- Outside corners, including specified miters.
- Glass-door jamb/head transitions and any dimensioned reveals.
- Niche opening perimeters and internal shelf fronts.
- Tile-to-slab, tile-to-mirror and other material joints.
- Floor finish transitions/thresholds.

Trace the actual finish boundary, not a swung door leaf or nearby wall/window line. Do not count wall/floor/ceiling abutments as exposed trim by default. A material joint or corner is not automatically metal trim. Keep miter LF separate from selected trim/profile LF; absent trim selections or return depths prevent a procurement-ready total. Do not apply a shower-corner specification to unrelated rooms without source support.

## Niche LF and SF

For a rectangular niche of width W, height H and finished depth D, all in feet:

- Opening perimeter = 2(W + H) LF.
- Back = W x H SF.
- Four returns = 2(W + H) x D SF.
- Each full-width internal shelf adds its exposed top and underside: usually 2 x W x D SF, subject to the actual detail.
- Shelf fascia = W x exposed thickness SF; shelf front = W LF.

Use actual shapes for nonrectangular niches. Assign back, returns, shelf and fascia materials independently when detailed. Do not assume a slab shelf is tile. If depth is absent, leave return SF unresolved or use a clearly provisional editable depth; there is no universal four-inch default. State whether height measures the entire opening or clear compartments so shelf thickness is handled consistently.

Field SF deducts the niche opening once. Schedule lining components separately; an informational niche total must not be added again to those components. Recalculating depth must change return/lining totals, not wall field area. Separate front/back/return physical planes even if their locator marks overlap on the elevation.
