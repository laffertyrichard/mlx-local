# Jurisdiction and applicable code workflow

## Proportional intake

For general education, state assumptions and proceed. For a project conclusion obtain relevant facts: exact address/APN/unit/building; current/proposed use and dwelling arrangement; stories/grade, documented size/construction type/occupant data; sprinkler/alarm status; work extent; occupied renovation; application/submission/approval/expiry/revision milestones; explicit approved code basis or exception; plans/CO/field evidence. Ask for only what the requested decision needs now.

Corroborate parcel jurisdiction and address using official GIS/assessor/municipal records. Mailing city and jurisdiction fields differ. Maps do not establish a survey, legal setback, entitlement or fire-service boundary. Resolve annexation or contradictory records through documented authority clarification. Independently identify fire authority/district and review responsibilities. Do not transfer one city's law throughout a county or confuse City of Maricopa with Maricopa County.

## Build the authority chain

Identify applicable federal/state law, local enactment and incorporated edition, amendments, chosen appendices and referenced-standard editions. Use official indexes and linked official codifiers; check codifier updates plus newer uncodified ordinances. Record adoption, effective date and permit-transition conditions separately. Submission, acceptance, issuance, expiry and revision dates can have different effects; a date alone is not an entitlement.

Administrative interpretations and policies have stated authority/scope. Checklists and forms locate issues; explanatory commentary and proposed changes are not automatically enacted provisions. Resolve a later ordinance versus older checklist conflict rather than silently choosing a convenient line. Search excerpts are discovery only. Open the body of the source; record retrieval date and contrary evidence. A future hearing or tentative adoption date does not prove a vote or effectiveness.

Do not transfer familiar numbers/sections across editions. If base text, amendment, exception or transition is unavailable, identify the exact missing source; provide conditional issue review and request an authorized excerpt or professional/AHJ verification. Do not buy access or contact officials implicitly.

## Select code path and scope triggers

Confirm adopted IRC/IBC scope from actual dwelling/building arrangement, height/stories, separate egress, use and locally adopted definitions/exceptions. Labels such as townhome, condo, rental, guest house or live/work are insufficient. Commercial TI/change of use needs deliberate occupancy/load/hazard classification even without wall changes.

Determine whether IEBC is adopted/incorporated and applicable, or the adopted IRC/local existing-building provisions govern. Identify repair/alteration/addition/relocation/change of occupancy using actual definitions and permissible method. Prescriptive, work-area and performance routes are distinct where adopted. Alteration levels are not universal across methods. Evaluate only substantiated triggers for untouched work, cumulative extent, structure, systems, energy, accessibility, fire/escape and hazards. Do not promise blanket grandfathering or impose a whole-house new-build requirement without a cited trigger.

## Parallel review streams

| Stream | Scope-dependent issues; attach actual governing evidence |
|---|---|
| Zoning/site | Lawful use, approved stipulations, setbacks/height/coverage, parking/signage/ADU, nonconformity, easement, flood/historic/hillside overlays. Setbacks differ from fire-separation distance. |
| Fire/egress | Occupancy/load, exit/route/door operation, rated assemblies/penetrations, alarms/sprinklers, sleeping-room rescue, stairs/guards/glazing and local pool rules. |
| Structure | Gravity/lateral load path, removal/openings/new loads/rooftop equipment, connections/foundations and concealed conditions; qualified design/field gate. |
| Mechanical | Ventilation/exhaust/termination, combustion air, service access, ducts/condensate, commercial cooking and energy interactions. |
| Plumbing/gas | Supply/drain/vent/capacity, fixture counts, hot water/backflow, gas loads/listings and lawful code path. |
| Electrical | Actual NEC/amendments, service/load/circuits, GFCI/AFCI/wet locations/working space, controls/listings/utilities. Narrow deferrals remain narrow. |
| Energy/green | Mandatory versus optional status, alteration scope, compliance route, envelope/systems/lighting and documentation/commissioning. |
| Accessibility | Adopted IBC/IEBC and referenced A117.1 edition; federal ADA and other housing/program/funding law separately. Private homes are not automatically ADA-covered. User access targets do not by themselves establish a legal mandate. Do not treat a percentage path-of-travel provision as a cap on all altered-element obligations or assume permits settle federal obligations. |
| Other/public-private | Scope-triggered health/food/hazard/demolition/stormwater/utilities and verified HOA/landlord/title conditions. Private approval does not replace government review. |

## Resolution and action limits

Field verify near-threshold clear dimensions with method/datum/tolerance; a nominal window or photograph is not net clear opening. Refer load-bearing, fire systems, service capacity, hazards and uncertain life safety to appropriate professionals. For credible active gas/electrical/fire/structural danger give immediate safety-oriented advice and qualified emergency/site response, not ordinary design troubleshooting.

AHJ gates: classification, use change, path, exemptions, alternative materials/methods, modifications, historic exceptions and disputed transitions. Draft one question tied to evidence/impact. Do not contact or submit without authority. Municipal source gaps remain unresolved; an attractive alternative does not close them.
