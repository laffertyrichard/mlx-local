# Critical Trade Coordination Notes

Document these intersections in internal notes when formatting adjacent trades.

## Electrical ↔ Other Trades
- Rough-in for HVAC equipment (disconnect, whip, dedicated circuits)
- Backing/blocking requests for heavy fixtures (coordinate with framing)
- Low voltage rough-in for AV/security (separate scope — flag if excluded)
- Under-cabinet lighting coordination with cabinet shop
- Appliance circuit requirements (verify amp/voltage per spec sheet)

## Plumbing ↔ Other Trades
- Fixture locations must match cabinet shop drawings
- Water heater venting coordination with HVAC/roofing
- Gas stub locations for cooktop, dryer, fireplace, BBQ
- Floor drain locations coordinated with tile layout
- Hose bib and irrigation stub locations coordinated with landscape

## HVAC ↔ Other Trades
- Duct routing vs. framing beam/joist layout
- Return air pathways through framing
- Exhaust fan locations coordinated with electrical
- Equipment pad locations coordinated with hardscape/landscape
- Range hood vent routing through roof or wall

## Framing ↔ Other Trades
- Backing/blocking for heavy items (TV mounts, grab bars, shower glass)
- Headers sized for window/door openings per schedule
- Beam pockets and connections per structural
- Soffits and bulkheads for duct/pipe routing

## Cabinets ↔ Other Trades
- Appliance dimensions and clearances verified before rough-in
- Electrical/plumbing rough-in locations per cabinet elevation drawings
- Countertop overhangs requiring support brackets
- Backsplash termination points and outlet box placement
- Filler strips and scribe requirements at walls

## Tile ↔ Other Trades
- Waterproofing responsibility (tile contractor vs. separate)
- Niche locations coordinated with framing blocking
- Floor drain locations set before mud bed
- Transition strips at flooring material changes
- Heated floor rough-in by electrician before tile

## Windows/Doors ↔ Other Trades
- Structural headers confirmed before order
- Flashing and waterproofing responsibility
- Interior trim/casing coordination
- Hardware prep for smart locks (low voltage rough-in)
- Screen and shade pocket coordination with framing
