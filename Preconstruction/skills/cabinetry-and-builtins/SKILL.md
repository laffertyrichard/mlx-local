# Cabinetry and built-ins that work

Scale the response to the request. Preserve the user's reference, activities and approved room envelope. A useful concept can proceed with labeled unknowns; a purchase or fabrication release needs resolved dependencies.

## Resolve what the parts mean

Identify the intended box/front system and visible cabinet language before composing a front grid. Give every filler, scribe, end skin, divider, pilaster, rail, toe and support a purpose, location and construction role. A decorative panel is not automatically a support; a member in a kneehole cannot be justified by calling it a filler. Do not delete a possible support before resolving its load path. Read [construction and finish](references/construction-and-finish.md) for component semantics, site tolerances, grain and moisture.

Record dimensions with units, datum, source/date and confidence. Separate nominal cabinet size, outside assembly, finished clear opening, blank stock and finished part dimensions. Reconcile run closure without counting allowances twice. Old plans and renderings do not establish current field geometry.

## Review four separate questions

- **Appearance:** Does the actual construction express the chosen reference? Inspect front rhythm, reveals, ends, grain and trim in closeups of every variant, including seated eye level and side returns.
- **Fit:** Do surveyed finished openings, body/approach space and installation/removal routes accommodate the assembly?
- **Operation:** Do real fronts, handles, hardware, drawers, appliance doors and users work throughout travel, including concurrent use? A closed reveal or final open pose is insufficient.
- **Support and coordination:** Are top/shelf loads, carcass joints, anchoring, services and ventilation resolved for the actual materials, hardware and substrate?

Read [operation and seated stations](references/operation-and-stations.md) for moving assemblies, mirrors, desks, makeup stations and appliances. Read [support and coordination](references/support-and-coordination.md) for countertop/cutout conditions, anchoring, media, laundry and window seats. Keep design preference, measured geometry, manufacturer instructions, adopted requirements and unresolved engineering distinct. No universal filler width, reveal, countertop span or anchor schedule follows from the word “cabinet.”

## Deliver a reviewable handoff

Show relevant plan, elevation and section relationships and the occupied/open states. Align a mirror and facial task lighting to the actual station and user; whole-run symmetry is a deliberate composition choice, not proof of usable alignment. Resolve an observed conflict with a coherent conditional option rather than hiding it in an open-question list.

For Blender/parts/takeoff deliverables read [handoff and QA](references/handoff-and-qa.md). Reuse established measurement and model mechanics and stable IDs. Authorization to design does not authorize live modeling, orders or changes to unrelated work. A render, part count or successful file readback proves neither fabrication readiness nor code/engineering approval.

Consult the [dated source registry](references/sources.md) for authority and access limits. Fetch the current selected product's regional manual before final sizing. Apply the project's specified standard/edition/grade; voluntary standards and NKBA recommendations do not establish local law. Use building-code-review when available for jurisdiction questions, or verify adoption and scope directly.
