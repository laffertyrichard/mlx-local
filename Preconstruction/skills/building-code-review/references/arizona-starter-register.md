# Arizona starter register — checked 2026-10-14

Dated evidence from the independent code researcher, preserved with conflicts and access limits. The installation agent separately reopened the four government code indexes and County active-process page on that date. This is not a maintained code database or a governing basis for an unidentified parcel. Reverify relevant pages, enacted text, amendments, applicability and permit transition for every project. If a newer official result differs, preserve both and resolve the difference.

All URLs were retrieved October 14, 2026. Dates below are the source’s expressly stated adoption/effective dates, not search-engine publication estimates. Page-level summaries establish a useful preliminary basis; adopted enactments, incorporated text, amendments, and project-specific transition evidence are needed for a final finding.

### Phoenix

**PHX-1 — Current official building-code index.** [Building Construction Codes](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/pdd/tools-resources/codes-ordinance/building-code.html). States adoption June 18, 2025, effective August 1, 2025; identifies Ordinance G-7397. Lists 2024 IBC/IRC/IMC/IFGC/IECC/IEBC/ISPSC, 2024 IPC and UPC, 2023 NEC, and optional 2024 IgCC, with local amendments. It lists selected appendices and elevator standards separately. Identifies ICC A117.1-2017 as scoped by amended 2024 IBC alongside the 2010 ADA Standards. Notice specifically defers enforcement of 2023 NEC 210.8(F) Exception 2 for HVAC equipment until March 1, 2027. That is a future date as of this memo, not a general electrical-code exemption. Retrieve the exact notice again before relying on it. The ordinance link did not open successfully through the research tool; do not represent this memo as an independent reading of G-7397.

**PHX-2 — Transition evidence.** [2024 PBCC Implementation](https://www.phoenix.gov/content/dam/phoenix/pddsite/documents/codes-ordinances/amendmentcodes/2024-building-code-implementation.pdf), one-page official PDF, no separate issue date visible. Initial submissions from August 1, 2025 generally use 2024. It describes limited 2018 exceptions through December 31, 2025, including development-review approvals, subsequent reviews, and official case-by-case allowances; all submissions after that date use 2024. Previously approved 2018 standard plans remained effective through July 31, 2026. Do not revive an expired general transition window; examine the actual permit, approval, expiration, resubmission, and any written exception.

**PHX-3 — Fire code is a separate check.** [Phoenix Fire Code](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/fire/safety-prevention/fire-code.html) and [adopted amendment packet](https://www.phoenix.gov/content/dam/phoenix/firesite/documents/2024%20Phoenix%20Fire%20Code%20Packet.pdf). Official fire page identifies the adopted 2024 IFC and amendments as effective February 17, 2026. It separately links a 2026 explanatory policy manual; distinguish explanations from adopted text. Do not use archived fire links on an older building-code section as the current basis.

**PHX-4 — Existing buildings.** [2024 IEBC local amendments](https://www.phoenix.gov/content/dam/phoenix/pddsite/documents/codes-ordinances/amendmentcodes/2024-iebc.pdf), linked from the current adopted-code index. Section 101.2.2 coordinates applicable existing-building work with Phoenix Fire Code Chapter 11; many administrative provisions are reserved to amended IBC Chapter 1. The PDF retains proposal-style headings and explanatory material: use the adopted-index linkage and ordinance to establish status; never treat every justification paragraph as a binding provision.

### Scottsdale

**SCO-1 — Current official index.** [Building Code Information](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/codes-and-ordinances/building-codes), page reports last update July 9, 2026. Lists 2021 IBC, IFC, IEBC, IPC, IMC, IFGC, ISPSC and 2020 NEC; labels building/fire effective January 1, 2023. IRC and IECC amendments are labeled effective January 7, 2023; mandatory IgCC effective July 1, 2023. Includes separate commercial TI energy and green checklists. These dates must not be collapsed into one date. The page’s current listing does not by itself determine a legacy project’s transition rights or a particular TI’s green-code scope.

**SCO-2 — Adoption history.** [Official code adoption history](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/docs/default-source/scottsdaleaz/codes---ordinances/building-codes/code-adoption-history.pdf?sfvrsn=12050a91_2), four-page PDF. Records Ordinance 4550 adoption September 20, 2022 for the listed 2021 building/existing/mechanical/plumbing/fuel-gas/pool codes and 2020 NEC; IFC Ordinance 4562 dated September 20, 2022; IECC and IgCC Ordinance 4576 dated December 6, 2022. This historical summary records adoption, not all implementation conditions. Its IRC history must be read alongside later residential/energy documents rather than used to override the current effective-date labels.

**SCO-3 — Existing-building adoption.** [2021 IEBC adoption PDF](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/docs/default-source/scottsdaleaz/codes---ordinances/building-codes/2021-international-existing-building-code-(iebc).pdf?sfvrsn=9f817774_2), Ordinance 4550 / Resolution 12504, Exhibit A, page 2, Scottsdale Revised Code §31-120. Incorporates 2021 IEBC by reference. The actual base code still must be consulted; the short municipal PDF is not the complete IEBC.

**SCO-4 — Residential project process.** [SFR Construction Process](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/planning-development/sfr-construction-process). Directs applicants to site-specific development standards, title reports and HOA restrictions, and describes inspections against approved plans and adopted codes. This supports a workflow with separate municipal, private restriction, and field-verification records; it does not establish any specific HOA restriction or approve a project.

### Fountain Hills

**FH-1 — Adopted code index.** [Adopted Codes](https://www.fountainhillsaz.gov/208/Adopted-Codes). States 2024 building codes effective September 1, 2025 and applicable to applications received on or after that date; lists 2023 NEC and 2024 IBC/IECC/IFC/IFGC/IMC/IPC/IRC. However, the summary names a “Uniform” swimming-pool code and an IRC Appendix G, whereas the ordinance below names ISPSC. Treat those details as a source conflict, especially for pools/appendices, not as interchangeable nomenclature. An IEBC adoption is not established by the list.

**FH-2 — Enactment and local amendments.** [Ordinance 25-06](https://www.fountainhillsaz.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12020/Ordinance-25-06-2024-building-codes-), 54-page official PDF. Page 1 states passed/adopted June 17, 2025; §2 makes it effective September 1, 2025. Exhibit A §7-1-1 names 2024 ISPSC, the code families above, and local amendments. §7-1-1(B) preserves residential sprinkler requirements through a reference to amended 2006 IFC adopted April 17, 2008. The enumerated adoption does not list IEBC; do not assert either independent IEBC adoption or total inapplicability of existing-building provisions without resolving incorporation/referrals with the adopted IBC and AHJ. §7-1-2(A)(2) changes IRC permit exemptions, illustrating why the unamended model exemption list cannot be used. Clarify conflicts before project advice.

### Unincorporated Maricopa County

**MC-1 — Current posted construction codes.** [Ordinances, Regulations and Codes](https://www.maricopa.gov/2271/Ordinances-Regulations-and-Codes). Records Board approval August 17, 2022 of 2018 IBC/IRC/IPC/IMC/IFGC/IEBC, optional 2018 IgCC and IECC, and 2017 NEC with AZBO/MAG amendments. [Adopted Regulations](https://www.maricopa.gov/2753/Adopted-Regulations) identifies September 16, 2022 effectiveness and grace through December 30, 2022. [Local Additions and Addenda](https://www.maricopa.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=6309) is the linked local amendment starting point. “Optional” in this County source does not establish the energy requirements of an incorporated city, a special program, or a particular funding arrangement.

**MC-2 — Pending 2026 change, not an adopted code basis.** [Active Regulatory Process](https://www.maricopa.gov/2778/Active-Regulatory-Process), opened live October 14, 2026, showed TA260001 at Step 5 with September 15, 2026 meeting; its Step 6 and recently-adopted sections had no listed item. Search excerpts showed an older August date, demonstrating why opened documents outrank snippets. [May 21, 2026 briefing](https://www.maricopa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/121081/Step-1---CM-Briefing-1) proposes 2024 codes/2023 NEC. [Tentative schedule](https://www.maricopa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/121082/Step-1---TA-Adoption-Process-Step-List) has planned hearing/adoption dates, including November 20, 2026, after this memo. The inconsistent tentative milestones do not prove a vote or effective date. Treat 2018/2017 as the current posted seed, with a mandatory freshness check for any live County project. A same-day hearing outcome was not established.

**MC-3 — Geographic scope.** [Residential Construction](https://www.maricopa.gov/1674/Residential-Construction) identifies County Planning and Development’s unincorporated-area role. County location does not mean County building jurisdiction. Never substitute the City of Maricopa’s adoption, a different government, for Maricopa County’s.
