# Standard Exclusions by Trade

These are review questions only. Carry an exclusion into a project only when supported by its governing scope or an explicit decision; never use the list as automatic exclusions.

Use these as starting points. Always review the specific vendor bid for additional exclusions and adjust to project scope.

## Demolition
- Landscaping, trees, shrubs removal
- Hazmat abatement (asbestos, lead paint)
- Unforeseen conditions behind walls/ceilings
- Utility disconnections by utility company

## Concrete
- Rock/caliche removal beyond specified depth
- Dewatering
- Soil remediation
- Engineering/structural design

## Framing
- Engineering and structural calculations (unless noted)
- Finish hardware
- Specialty connectors beyond standard

## HVAC
- Ductwork modifications beyond specified scope
- Electrical connections (by electrician)
- Specialty ventilation (range hoods, bath fans unless noted)
- Gas piping (verify — sometimes by plumber)
- Thermostats and controls (verify inclusion)

## Plumbing
- Fixture supply (unless noted in bid)
- Water heater (unless noted)
- Main line work beyond specified distance from structure
- Gas piping for appliances (verify trade responsibility)
- Fire sprinkler system (separate trade)

## Electrical
- Fixture supply (unless noted)
- Low voltage / AV systems
- Solar prep (unless noted)
- Panel upgrades beyond specified scope
- Utility company fees and meter upgrades

## Flooring
- Furniture moving
- Floor prep beyond standard leveling
- Transitions to existing flooring (unless noted)
- Stair nosings and specialty trim
- Moisture testing/mitigation

## Tile
- Waterproofing (verify — sometimes separate)
- Fixture cutouts beyond standard
- Specialty patterns requiring layout approval
- Grout sealing

## Roofing
- Structural repairs/sheathing replacement
- Skylights and penetrations (unless noted)
- Gutters and downspouts (unless noted)
- Solar panel coordination

## Drywall
- Framing corrections
- Insulation (separate trade)
- Specialty textures beyond standard
- Sound attenuation (unless noted)

## Stucco/Exterior
- Structural repairs
- Window/door installation
- Waterproofing membrane (verify inclusion)
- Color/finish mockup approval process

## Windows & Doors
- Structural headers/framing modifications
- Finish hardware (interior doors)
- Specialty glazing beyond specified
- Screen doors/window screens (verify)

## Cabinets
- Countertops (separate trade)
- Hardware beyond standard allowance
- Appliance panels (verify)
- Toe kick lighting

## Countertops
- Plumbing disconnection/reconnection
- Electrical for under-mount lighting
- Backsplash (unless noted)
- Sink and faucet supply
