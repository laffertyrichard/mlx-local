# Sources and access limits

Primary source leads from the independent code researcher, inspected 2026-10-14 unless its memo explicitly recorded an access limitation. The [Arizona register](arizona-starter-register.md) contains dated adoption and transition findings plus exact local document links. Reopen applicable sources at use; no seed entry establishes a project's jurisdiction or legal requirements by itself.

| Source | Role and limit |
|---|---|
| [ICC adoption resources](https://www.iccsafe.org/advocacy/code-adoption-resources/) | Model-code adoption orientation; locate enactment rather than assuming publication = law. |
| [2024 IRC Chapter 1](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2024P1/chapter-1-scope-and-administration) | Model scope R101.2 and appendix adoption R101.2.1 as a dated example. Check actual locally adopted edition/amendments; exceptions need exact text. |
| [ICC 2021 IEBC commentary sample, Chapter 3](https://shop.iccsafe.org/media/wysiwyg/material/3560S21-Sample.pdf) | Orientation to distinct methods, not enacted text or automatically governing edition. |
| [Access Board ADA scoping guide](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-1-using-the-ada-standards/) | Housing coverage and federal/local distinctions; not automatic private-home applicability. Reopened by installation agent. |
| [DOJ 2010 Standards §§201–202](https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/) | Alteration/addition scoping, subject to actual legal applicability; altered elements and path of travel are different obligations. |
| [DOJ Title III primer](https://www.ada.gov/resources/title-iii-primer/) | Federal orientation to altered facilities versus barrier removal; do not generalize a percentage rule. |
| [Maricopa Assessor parcel-search help](https://maps.mcassessor.maricopa.gov/help/t_search.html) | Parcel discovery aid. |
| [Assessor parcel service](https://gis.mcassessor.maricopa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Parcels/MapServer/0) | Jurisdiction and mailing-address fields are separate; mapping is not a legal survey or fire-authority determination. |
| [Scottsdale Map Center](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/maps) | Official boundary/parcel leads, not proof that an unidentified listing is in Scottsdale. |

Government code indexes and official linked enactments/codifiers establish local leads; publisher text establishes base text only after incorporation/applicability is verified. Manufacturer instructions/listings have specific product/installation scope. Trade articles, snippets and generated summaries are discovery tools only.

Do not attribute unseen content to inaccessible code pages, paid tables or a broken municipal link. Preserve exact missing edition/section/document in the evidence matrix; request an authorized excerpt or qualified verification. No paid access, manual collection, municipal contact or permit submission was authorized by skill installation. Paraphrase narrowly and respect quotation limits.

Important outstanding research limits: Phoenix G-7397 itself was not independently read by the researcher; the current index identifies it. Fountain Hills summary/ordinance pool terminology differs and independent IEBC adoption is not established. County update milestones are proposals until final adoption/effectiveness is verified. Municipal page summaries cannot close these gaps or establish every amendment.
