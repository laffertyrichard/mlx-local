# Synthetic fixtures

All files in this directory are invented test data generated by `../tools/generate_fixtures.py`; none were taken from customer drawings. Geometry/records are hand-authored ground truth, not model extraction results or real human approvals.

- `synthetic-plan.pdf`: one-page text/vector evidence with a 12 × 10 ft room and fixed 2 × 3 ft island deduction, explicit unknowns and a floor-material conflict.
- `synthetic-plan.svg`: simplified illustrative source counterpart, not a byte-identical PDF conversion.
- `area-plane.json`: unchanged existing area-helper schema, top-left PDF point frame, 18 points/ft; gross 120 SF − deduction 6 SF = candidate net 114 SF.
- `revision.json`: proposed new domain envelope; all checks and approvals are pending, quantities provisional/retained/unresolved. `synthetic-revision-note.txt` establishes the competing material, without resolving precedence.
- `extra-geometry.json`: hand-authored LF/count/crop examples; open 12 + 10 ft route and two physical fixtures with one repeated appearance. These extra records test arithmetic/identity independently; they are not represented as extracted from the floor diagram.
- `adversarial-cases.json`: topology failures, calibration conflict, missing height and stale-resume scenarios. Only checks explicitly named in the validation report have run; this is not an app acceptance report.
- `synthetic-overlay.svg`: illustrative net mask with a clear island hole, generated solely for fixture review. It is not a reusable source-overlay exporter or approved takeoff.
- `scope-*-draft.txt`: synthetic client/internal text with quantity references and disclosed unknowns; no pricing. They are fixed examples, not outputs of a local model.
- `expected.json`: ground truth and release hold, not a claim that the application enforces it.

Do not scale exported fixture views. Real projects need their own calibration and review. Core regeneration overwrites only this named fixture set. Optional scanned-PDF/crop examples, if included, are derivatives of this synthetic PDF and record their transforms/hash in `derived-fixtures.json`.
