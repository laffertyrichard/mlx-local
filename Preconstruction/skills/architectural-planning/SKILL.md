# Architectural planning with coordinated consequences

Turn the user's brief into testable spatial options and explain what each option changes elsewhere. Keep concept usefulness and evidence certainty separate; missing technical evidence holds its dependent claim, not every reversible planning step.

## Establish the decision

Identify building/use, location, project scope, stage/deliverable, budget/disruption, users, retained work and priorities. Define observable outcomes and relevant adjacencies: must connect, near, separated or flexible. Read [planning principles](references/planning-principles.md) for programming, circulation, proportion, privacy, daylight and meaningful alternatives.

Register drawings/photos/measurements by source/revision/view, units/datum and confidence. Separate field-verified conditions, record drawings, proposals and placeholders; record contradictions and targeted verification. Label claims **verified applicable requirement**, **published recommendation**, **design judgment** or **assumption/unresolved**. An official source alone does not establish applicability. Do not infer current as-builts from an unscaled photo or hide conflicting geometry through rescaling.

## Compare and coordinate

Where useful compare distinct intervention strategies, including retaining/reconfiguring before enlarging. Use the same evidence basis; explain gains, compromises, enabling work and uncertain cost drivers. Recommend against the user's priorities without invented scoring precision.

Test occupied paths and operations, noisy/quiet adjacency, sightlines, usable wall lengths and actual furniture scale. Coordinate **plan, section and elevation**: ceilings/soffits/headroom, floor levels, structure/service depth, opening heads/sills, roof/shading and threshold interfaces. A clear plan aisle does not solve an overhead obstruction. Borrowed light, solar heat, view and a legally required opening are different tests.

Read [remodel coordination](references/remodel-coordination.md) when changing walls/openings, envelope, wet areas or services. Trace conceptual gravity/lateral load paths and water/air/thermal relationships; identify structural/MEP/assembly consequences and qualified review. Never size a beam from appearance or conceal unknown drain/slab conditions. Identify fatal use/escape/level problems before visual polishing; keep conditional alternatives explicitly conditional.

## Handoff at the right maturity

For adopted requirements or permit questions, use building-code-review when available; otherwise establish parcel authority, applicable code path, edition/amendments and permit-date basis before a binding statement. Private homes are not automatically ADA-covered; user access goals remain valuable independently.

Read [workflow integration](references/workflow-integration.md) for local Blender and takeoff routing, stable IDs, approved-envelope preservation and maturity gates. Use the available interior-design skill for interior fit/material/light decisions. These routes do not expand scope or authorization.

Deliver the recommended concept, meaningful alternatives, necessary dimensioned plan/sections/elevations, open conflicts and next verification actions in the requested form. See [sources](references/sources.md) for research provenance and access limits. A rendering, owner visual approval, saved-file test or detailed-looking model does not establish field, structural, code, fabrication or permit approval.
