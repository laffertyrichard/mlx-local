# Evidence matrix and handoff

Use a compact table for the requested issues, expanding only as complexity warrants. Missing fields are marked unknown with consequences, not filled from a familiar city or model edition.

## Project and source basis

**Project record:** ID/address/APN; building/zoning/fire authority and jurisdiction evidence/date; existing/proposed use/building facts/confidence; scope/code path/method; permit milestones; edition by discipline; enactment, amendments, appendices and referenced standards; transition evidence; conflicts; review date.

**Source record:** S-ID, issuer/title/exact URL, ordinance/document number, status (adopted/proposed/guidance/interpretation/permit record), adoption/effective/transition dates and conditions, last amended/page updated, retrieval timestamp, edition, precise section/page/table, short paraphrase, what it establishes/does not establish, stale/conflict flag.

## Finding contract

| Field | Required substance for an actual issue |
|---|---|
| F-ID + location | Room/object/sheet/detail/revision and actual question |
| Project evidence | Approved drawing, proposed revision, field observation/measurement or assumption, each with its source/date |
| Governing basis | Code/standard and edition, exact section/table/exception, local enactment, applicable amendment/appendix and authority |
| Applicability | Why property/use/work/permit facts fall within the provision; unresolved interpretation flagged |
| Test/calculation | Units, dimensions/datums/tolerance, inputs/source, measurement basis and assumptions |
| Result + gap | One bounded state, consequence and specific evidence still needed |
| Resolution | Responsible discipline/decision-maker, next action, Q-ID, date and closure evidence |

Permitted result states: **supported on supplied documents**, **apparent conflict**, **not demonstrated**, **not applicable with reason**, **requires AHJ interpretation**, **requires field verification**, **requires professional design**. More than one dependency can be linked. Document-supported is not installed compliance or permit certainty. Do not use a binary pass for an unmeasured/unresolved condition.

Keep approved documents, proposed revision and observed conditions separate. A photo cannot establish concealed wiring/reinforcement/waterproofing/ratings/capacity/legal status. Preserve source revisions and superseded findings; recalculate dependent work when an answer changes its basis.

For an unresolved jurisdiction/adoption-basis review, also populate a compact **review-lane/status** table: building/existing-work path; zoning; fire authority/triggers; structure; plumbing/gas; mechanical; electrical; energy/green; legal accessibility versus owner access targets; and private HOA/landlord/title restrictions. Each row gets scope relevance, known/unknown evidence and responsible next reviewer. “Not demonstrated; applicability needs scope/authority facts” is useful when evidence is missing. A private restriction remains conditional until an actual document establishes it; its row does not invent an HOA or approval requirement. For narrower technical questions include only affected lanes.

## Concise deliverable

Lead with the decision and basis, then a scope-matched matrix, highest-consequence gaps and next actions. Separate immediate safety, permit blockers, costly rework and documentation issues. An AHJ question should identify facts, enactment/section conflict, requested interpretation and decision consequence; it is a draft, not a sent inquiry.

Reuse project source/object/decision/question IDs for architectural/interior/Blender handoffs. For quantity changes use available plan-area-takeoff registers/manifest; do not duplicate measuring or promote unverified model dimensions into field/code findings. Code resolution updates dependent design/scope, but design preference and permit approval retain their separate provenance.
