# Scope-matched multi-trade review

Review the scope of work before measuring. Inventory every received sheet by actual title and PDF page, including schedules/details. Mark reviewed, applicable, missing referenced source, and no quantity established; a reviewed page does not imply every trade on it is fully quantified. If the user requests a one-page trial, preserve its approved basis when expanding.

Convert scope paragraphs into stable rows linked to quantity IDs, source pages, treatment and unresolved issues. Compare contractor and designer/client scopes explicitly. Preserve stated exclusions, completed work, owner/client work, optional phases and procurement responsibility. Respect supplied document precedence; do not assume date alone resolves contradictions. Resolve a scope row as measured, provisional/partial, unquantified, excluded or option, rather than forcing a numeric quantity.

Group work into only the requested trades. For each, reconcile scope items to drawing evidence and quantities; every unresolved scope item remains visible. Record alternatives, parent totals and subsets as relationships so summaries do not add them together. Round displayed quantities without suggesting scan-derived precision is field accuracy.

For depicted MEP scope, link equipment/fixture/device counts by physical item across plans and schedules. Distinguish existing, relocated and new items and equipment capacities from counts. Do not infer concealed routing, complete wire/pipe/duct lengths, circuits, service upgrades or complete electrical scope from incomplete drawings. A schedule/detail shows an assembly's requirements, not how many occurrences exist. Keep unavailable design information open.

For existing-service mapping or reuse, apply the infrastructure register and overlay procedure routed from SKILL.md. For removals, relocations and penetrations, apply the demolition/restoration procedure to link related operations without inventing extent or responsibility. For openings, assemblies, stairs and sitework, load the dedicated scope reference before claiming trade coverage.

A useful package contains a first-tab trade summary, detailed quantities and inputs, scope matrix, open-issue register, sheet inventory and quantity-keyed overlays. Each overlay legend shows ID, description, quantity and unit; repeated detail views repeat the reference quantity without increasing totals. Show derived wall/ceiling SF alongside the measured LF/projection geometry that supports it. Preserve approved quantity IDs and report corrections consistently through all artifacts.
