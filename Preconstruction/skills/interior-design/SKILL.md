# Interior design that works in use

Make the recommendation serve the people, activities and verified space before styling it. Scale the work to the request: a quick critique needs concrete findings and fixes, not a whole-project report.

## Work from the brief and evidence

Identify activities and simultaneous users, retained pieces, functional/access needs, priorities, budget/disruption and maintenance tolerance. Ask only questions that change the next decision; continue useful conditional concepts while measurements remain open.

Label material inputs and claims: **verified condition**, **applicable requirement**, **published recommendation**, **design judgment**, or **assumption/unresolved**. Record source/date, units and datum for dimensions. Existing drawings, unscaled photos and renderings do not establish current as-builts. Resolve source authority by the decision being made, not simply newest date. Read [fit and coordination](references/fit-and-coordination.md) for layouts, source conflicts, product fit and room-specific tests.

## Test use, then compose

Show physical footprints, operation envelopes and human-use/approach envelopes separately, plus circulation. Check occupied seating, drawers/doors/appliances open, handles, delivery/removal, service access and the narrowest pinch point. Identify the facing planes and local axes before clearance arithmetic. A bounding-box fit is insufficient. If a critical operation fails, propose a meaningful change; an attractive score cannot override it. Mark uncertain performance unverified.

Compare distinct useful options against the user's priorities, then recommend one with its trade-off and what evidence could change it. Do not impose an island, open plan, symmetry, neutral palette or a style formula. Read [principles](references/principles.md) for composition, materials, light, acoustics and adaptability. Use real samples and user trials where appearance or comfort cannot be established in a model.

## Coordinate and deliver

For physical reconfiguration use architectural-planning when available. For binding dimensions, exemptions or permit questions use building-code-review when available; otherwise verify actual jurisdiction/adoption before a requirement claim. Published guidelines and owner access goals are separate from legal minima; private homes are not automatically ADA-covered. Fetch current model-specific installation instructions before final product fit or purchase recommendations. Do not claim unseen paid-standard tables or hard-code a “latest” NKBA edition. See the [source registry](references/sources.md).

For model/estimate handoffs read [workflow integration](references/workflow-integration.md). Preserve approved envelopes, retained equipment, stable decision/source/question IDs and uncertainty. Route execution to available Blender skills and measurement to plan-area-takeoff; their availability and authorization must be checked.

Deliver the recommendation, key dimensions/operating tests, material/light intent and prioritized open decisions in the form requested. Inspect consistency across plan, relevant elevations/sections and imagery; show normal occupied use. A concept, visual approval or successful model readback does not establish installation, engineering, field or code approval.
